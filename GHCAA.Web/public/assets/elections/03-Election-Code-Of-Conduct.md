# 1. Purpose

This Code establishes ethical standards governing all election-related activities.

Every participant shall conduct themselves honestly, respectfully, professionally, impartially and in accordance with the Constitution.

---

# 2. Fundamental Principles

Every election shall uphold

• Integrity

• Transparency

• Neutrality

• Fairness

• Equality

• Respect

• Accountability

• Confidentiality

• Non-political Character

• Non-discrimination

---

# 3. General Obligations

Every participant shall

Act honestly.

Respect fellow members.

Follow the Constitution.

Follow Election Regulations.

Comply with lawful directions of the Election Commission.

Protect Association property.

Protect confidential information.

Avoid conflicts of interest.

Report misconduct immediately.

---

# 4. Election Commission Conduct

Election Commissioners shall

Remain politically neutral.

Remain independent.

Avoid personal bias.

Treat every candidate equally.

Avoid private meetings with candidates.

Maintain confidentiality.

Document every decision.

Provide written reasons for major decisions.

Maintain complete election records.

Commissioners shall never

Campaign.

Accept gifts.

Accept promises.

Discuss confidential voting information.

Influence voters.

Use Association resources for personal benefit.

---

# 5. Candidate Conduct

Candidates shall

Respect opponents.

Respect voters.

Conduct issue-based campaigns.

Follow campaign schedule.

Submit truthful information.

Accept election results.

Candidates shall not

Offer money.

Offer gifts.

Offer employment.

Promise Association resources.

Threaten members.

Harass voters.

Spread false information.

Defame opponents.

Misuse Association logo.

Misuse Association funds.

Use religious institutions.

Use political organizations.

Use hate speech.

Use discriminatory language.

Damage campaign materials.

Interfere with polling.

Interfere with counting.

---

# 6. Campaign Rules

Campaign begins

On official campaign start date.

Campaign ends

24 hours before polling.

During silence period

Candidates shall not

Campaign publicly.

Publish advertisements.

Send campaign SMS.

Send campaign emails.

Use loudspeakers.

Distribute leaflets.

Organize campaign meetings.

Campaign materials shall clearly identify

Candidate

Position

Contact Information

Campaign expenditures shall be recorded.

Commission may audit campaign expenses.

---

# 7. Member Conduct

Members shall

Vote independently.

Maintain order.

Respect polling officials.

Report irregularities.

Members shall not

Vote twice.

Assist impersonation.

Damage election materials.

Threaten candidates.

Disrupt polling.

Reveal another person's vote.

---

# 8. Observer Conduct

Observers shall

Remain neutral.

Observe silently.

Document facts.

Report through official channels.

Observers shall not

Campaign.

Influence voters.

Give instructions.

Interfere with polling.

Handle ballots.

Touch electronic systems.

Discuss unofficial results.

---

# 9. Polling Officials

Polling Officials shall

Verify identity.

Maintain secrecy.

Secure ballots.

Protect ballot boxes.

Record incidents.

Maintain professional behaviour.

Officials shall never

Suggest candidates.

Influence votes.

Discuss unofficial counts.

Leave polling station unattended.

Destroy election materials.

---

# 10. Technical Administrators

Technical Administrators shall

Maintain system availability.

Maintain cybersecurity.

Maintain confidentiality.

Maintain backups.

Maintain audit logs.

Report technical incidents immediately.

Administrators shall never

Modify votes.

Delete logs.

Reveal passwords.

Share OTPs.

Access votes unnecessarily.

Disable audit logging.

Manipulate timestamps.

---

# 11. Media & Public Communication

Only Chief Election Commissioner or authorized spokesperson may

Publish official announcements.

Release unofficial information is prohibited.

Rumours shall not be published.

Election results shall not be announced before certification.

---

# 12. Social Media

Candidates may use

Facebook

LinkedIn

WhatsApp

Email

Official websites

Campaign content shall

Remain respectful.

Avoid misinformation.

Avoid personal attacks.

Avoid fake accounts.

Avoid anonymous campaigns.

Commission may require removal of unlawful content.

---

# 13. Conflict of Interest

Every Election Official shall disclose

Financial interests.

Family relationships.

Business relationships.

Political affiliations.

Professional conflicts.

Persons with conflicts shall withdraw immediately.

Failure to disclose constitutes misconduct.

---

# 14. Gifts & Hospitality

Election participants shall not

Accept cash.

Accept gifts.

Accept entertainment.

Accept travel.

Accept accommodation.

Accept discounts.

Exceptions

Token refreshments provided equally to all participants.

# 15. Confidentiality

Confidential Information includes

Passwords.

Audit logs.

Voting records.

Member information.

Technical documentation.

Complaint investigations.

Draft decisions.

# 18. Disciplinary Actions

Commission may impose

Advice.

Warning.

Written Warning.

Show Cause Notice.

Campaign Suspension.

Candidate Disqualification.

Polling Removal.

Official Removal.

Referral to Tribunal.

Referral to Executive Committee.

Referral to Law Enforcement where applicable.

Serious violations may invalidate election results if they materially affected the election outcome.

Evidence.

Applicable Rule.

Right of Appeal.

Appeal Authority.

• Interfering with observers

• Obstructing investigations
