## Declaration

We jointly certify that the polling process has concluded in accordance with the
Constitution and the Election Regulations.

To the best of our knowledge, there has been no unauthorised access, substitution,
removal, insertion or tampering of ballot papers or election materials before the
commencement of counting.

After signing this certificate, objections regarding the integrity of the polling
process or the custody of the ballot box shall only be entertained upon presentation of
credible evidence of fraud, tampering, coercion or other material irregularity.
