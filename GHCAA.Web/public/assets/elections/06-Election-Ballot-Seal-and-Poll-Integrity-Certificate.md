Declaration

We jointly certify that the polling process has concluded in accordance with the Constitution and Election Regulations.

To the best of our knowledge, there has been no unauthorized access, substitution, removal, insertion, or tampering of ballot papers or election materials before commencement of counting.

After signing this certificate, objections regarding the integrity of the polling process or custody of the ballot box shall only be entertained upon presentation of credible evidence of fraud, tampering, coercion, or other material irregularity.
