ARTICLE I: MEMBERSHIP ELIGIBILITY & CLASSIFICATION

Section 1.01: Definitive Eligibility

Registration is exclusively open to "eligible alumni" of Government Haraganga College (established 18 Dec 1938). To qualify, the Applicant must prove they are:

  • a) Graduate Member: An individual who has successfully completed a degree (HSC, Pass, Honors, Masters) from the institution.
  • b) Associate Member: An individual who was a regular student of the institution for a period of at least two full academic years but did not complete a degree.
  • c) Right of Verification: The Association and the Adhoc Committee reserve the absolute right to verify an Applicant's academic history with the official records of Government Haraganga College. Membership is contingent upon successful verification.
Section 1.02: Truth in Application

The Applicant certifies that all information provided—including name, batch year, contact information, and professional details—is accurate and complete. Any misrepresentation, falsehood, or omission discovered at any time is grounds for immediate membership revocation and expulsion from the Association without refund.

ARTICLE II: ORGANIZATIONAL NATURE & MEMBER CONDUCT

Section 2.01: Core Values and Enduring Vision

By registering, the Member commits to upholding the principles defined in the Constitution’s Preamble: lifelong connection, ethical conduct, collective progress, and service to the institution and society.

Section 2.02: Mandatory Code of Conduct

All members are bound by the nature of the Association (Article I, Section 3). During all events (physical and digital), internal communication, and representation of the Association, members must uphold a strict code of conduct:

  • a) Apolitical Nature: Partisan political activities, campaigning, and debates are strictly prohibited.
  • b) Non-religious Nature: The Association is secular; promoting religious dogma or discrimination is prohibited.
  • c) Inclusivity: Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or hate speech based on race, gender, religion, or background is a zero-tolerance offense.
  • d) Professionalism: Members must interact with each other, faculty, and current students with respect and professional courtesy.
Section 2.03: Digital and Social Media Policy

These T&C extend to all official Association online platforms (WhatsApp groups, Facebook, Website Forum).

  • a) Prohibited Content: Spamming, commercial advertising, misinformation, or content that damages the reputation of the College or Association is strictly forbidden.
  • b) Responsibility: Each member is personally responsible for the content they post. The Association reserves the right to remove non-compliant content and restrict a member’s access to these platforms.

ARTICLE III: FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS & CONTRIBUTIONS

Section 3.01: Registration and Subscription Fees

Membership may require the payment of a one-time registration fee and/or annual subscription fees, as determined by the Constitution and the Adhoc Committee.

  • a) Non-Refundable Nature: All fees are non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances, including membership revocation.
  • b) Active Status: Only members who are current on their financial obligations ("Active Members") are entitled to vote, hold office, or receive member-exclusive benefits.
Section 3.02: Donations and Philanthropy (Fitantrofic)

Consistent with its nature, the Association encourages voluntary donations. These contributions will be used strictly for education, cultural preservation, and philanthropic support (Article I, Section 3).

  • a) General Prohibition: Members are strictly prohibited from using the Association or College name, logo, or reputation for personal gain, commercial endorsement, or political purposes.
  • b) Permitted Use: The use of the logo for official Association communication or merchandise requires formal written permission from the Adhoc Committee and the Institution.

Section 5.01: The "Integrating Networks" Motto

Members have the right to log in to their profiles and control the visibility of certain personal information (like direct contact details) to other members.
