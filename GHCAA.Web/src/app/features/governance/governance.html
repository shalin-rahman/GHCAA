Governance Pillars

Our association is built on foundations of integrity and shared values.

⚖️

Political Neutrality

The association maintains strict political and religious neutrality to ensure an inclusive environment for all alumni regardless of their affiliations.

🤝

Life-Long Connection

Governance is focused on long-term connection, professional mentorship, and supporting the college's legacy across generations.

📈

Transparency

All financial records and meeting minutes are archived for member inspection at the annual general meetings to ensure complete accountability.
