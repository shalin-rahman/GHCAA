# Election Forms — Appendices

These appendices govern how the forms in Parts I and II are signed, moved and kept. They
are read alongside the forms; they are not themselves forms to be completed.

Form numbers follow the Regulations, as decided on 2026-10-20. Section Annex B of `12-Election-Officials-Handbook.md` lists all 50 forms.

## Appendix A — Mandatory Signature Matrix

A form is not valid until every signature marked Required has been given. Optional means
the signature is taken if the person is present, and their absence is recorded on the form.

| Poll Opening (ER-23) | Required | | Required | Optional | Required |

## Appendix B — Chain of Custody

Election materials pass through the following documents in order. Each step is evidenced
by the form named against it, and no step may be skipped.

1. Election materials issued
2. Material Receipt Register (ER-22A)
3. Polling Officer verification
4. Poll Opening Certificate (ER-23)
5. Voting
6. Poll Closing Certificate (ER-24)
7. Ballot Box Sealing Certificate (ER-25)
8. Counting Authorisation (ER-25A)
9. Ballot Box Opening Certificate (ER-25B)
10. Vote Counting Sheets (ER-27)
11. Independent Tally Sheets (ER-27A)
12. Result Certification (ER-29)
13. Election Archive Register (ER-35)
14. Executive Committee Handover (ER-34)
15. Election Closure Certificate (ER-35A)

## Appendix C — Record Retention

Every record below is kept for ten years from the declaration of the result, as Regulation Article 17
sets. No record has a shorter or an open-ended period.

| Record | Minimum Retention |
|---------|-------------------|
| Ballot Papers | 10 years |
| Result Sheets | 10 years |
| Election Certificates | 10 years |
| Complaints | 10 years |
| Appeals | 10 years |
| Tribunal Decisions | 10 years |
| Registers | 10 years |
| Election Reports | 10 years |
| Handover Records | 10 years |
