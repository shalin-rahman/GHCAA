# Response log

One copy per participant, saved into `responses/` as `P01.md`, `P02.md` and so on. The reference is
the only identifier. Do not write the participant's name anywhere in this folder, and do not keep a
separate sheet mapping references to names: in an association this small that sheet is the whole of
the confidentiality promise, and the study does not need it.

Where the office itself identifies the person, which it does for the President, the Treasurer and the
General Secretary, the office is recorded because the analysis depends on it, and §4.9 already
discloses that those three took part. Nothing else that would identify a participant goes in here.
