# Task script: Treasurer

Five tasks, about twenty-five minutes. Run on whatever device the Treasurer would actually use for
this work, and note which.

The Treasurer's work is the part of the Association the platform changes most, because it replaces a
paper and message-thread record with a ledger. These tasks are written against that, not against the
screens.

---

## Task 1. Confirm a payment

**Start state:** signed in as an administrator, with at least one payment declared and unverified.

> A member says they have paid their subscription and has sent you a receipt. Check it and record
> that the Association has received the money.

**Success:** the declaration located, the proof image opened and read, and the payment verified
against the correct member and the correct due.

**Watch for:** whether the proof is legible on their device; whether they can tell what the member
claims to have paid against what is owed; whether anything on the screen would let them verify a
payment without looking at the proof. That last one is a control question, and if the answer is yes
it is a finding for §8.9, not a usability observation.

---

## Task 2. Reject one

**Start state:** as above, a second declaration pending, with a reference that does not match.

> This one does not match anything in the Association's account. Deal with it.

**Success:** the declaration rejected with a reason the member will be able to act on.

**Watch for:** whether they can reject at all without deleting; whether they expect the member to be
told automatically; what reason they write, and whether the field lets them write it.

---

## Task 3. Who has not paid

> The Executive Committee meets next week and will ask you who is behind on their subscription. Find
> out.

**Success:** the members in arrears identified, and the Treasurer confident enough in the number to
say it aloud in a meeting.

**Watch for:** whether they trust the figure. A Treasurer who finds the number but says they would
check it against their own notes before quoting it has told you the platform has not yet replaced the
notes, which is the real measure for this task and belongs in §12.10.

---

## Task 4. Set what is owed

> Subscription rates for the coming year have been agreed. Set them up so members are charged
> correctly.

**Success:** the fee configuration changed to match what they were told, with the effect on existing
members understood.

**Watch for:** whether they understand who the change applies to and from when, and whether a new
rate quietly applies to months already gone.

---

## Task 5. Show the money

> Prepare what you would show the Executive Committee about the Association's receipts this year.

**Success:** the ledger or report reached and something produced they would be willing to present.

**Watch for:** what is missing from it. DC-14 requires financial records maintained and reported to
the membership, and the gap between what the platform produces and what the Treasurer would actually
present is a requirement finding, not a usability one. Write down what they say they would add by
hand.

---
