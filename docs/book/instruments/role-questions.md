# Role questions

Asked after the SUS form, never before it. These are open questions and the answers are the
stakeholder evidence of §12.7. Write them down in the participant's own words. A paraphrase written up
afterwards loses the part that was worth having.

Ask the common questions of everyone. Then the set for the office.

Do not ask a leading question, and do not defend the platform when the answer is critical. If a
participant says something is wrong that you know to be a misunderstanding, note the
misunderstanding and move on. A member who misunderstands the platform is a finding about the
platform.

## Common, all participants

1. What did you use to do this before, and what did that cost you in time or trouble?
2. Is there anything you would still do the old way even now the platform exists? Why?
3. What would stop you using this?
4. Is there anything here you would not trust?
5. If the platform disappeared tomorrow, what would the Association lose?

## Ordinary member

6. Was there anything you were asked for that you would rather not give?
7. When you told the Association you had paid, did you expect anything to happen next? Did it?
8. Would you look at the constitution here, or would you ask someone?

## Treasurer

6. Where does your record of money live now, and would this replace it or sit beside it?
9. What could go wrong with confirming payments this way, and what would you want in place before
   you relied on it?
10. Is there anything here that would let a payment be recorded that had not been received?
11. What would you need to show the Executive Committee that the platform does not yet give you?

## General Secretary

6. What do you have to do for an application that the platform does not know about?
7. When you admit someone, what are you actually deciding? Which parts of that could a computer do,
   and which parts should it never do?
8. How would you know an application had been waiting too long?
9. What do you write down that never goes anywhere near this platform, and why?

## President

6. Which of the Association's decisions could safely be made by the software, and which must stay
   with people, whatever the software could be made to do?
7. If a member disputed a decision the platform recorded, would the record settle it?
8. Does putting the constitution and the election rules on a public website change how members treat
   them?
9. The ballot, the count and the declaration stay with the Election Commission and outside the
   platform. Is that the right line? Would you move it?
10. If the platform's maintainer stepped away, what would happen to the Association?
11. Is there anything about running the Association through software that you are uneasy about, that
    I have not asked?

The last question in each set is the one most likely to produce something the dissertation cannot
get any other way. Leave time for it and do not fill the silence.
