# Observation checklist

For the person running the session, filled in during it, not afterwards.

Participant reference: ____________  Office or ordinary member: ____________
Date: ____________  Device: phone / tablet / computer  Network: mobile data / wifi
Environment: pre-production / production

## Per task

One row per task. Copy the block for each.

```
Task number:
Start time:               End time:              Duration:
Outcome:   completed unaided / completed after wrong turns / abandoned / believed complete but wrong
First place they looked:
Wrong turns, in order:
Points where they stopped and read:
Anything they said aloud, verbatim:
Errors the platform showed them, and whether the message helped:
Did they ask for help, and what for:
```

Mark "believed complete but wrong" carefully. It is the most important outcome on the list and the
easiest to miss, because the participant is satisfied and moves on. Check the end state yourself
before starting the next task.

## Across the session

- Anything slow enough that they noticed, and what they did while waiting.
- Anything that looked wrong on their screen size.
- Any point where they were unsure whether an action had saved.
- Any point where they were unsure who would see what they entered.
- Anything they did with the platform that was not one of the tasks.
- Anything they expected the platform to do that it does not.

## Your own notes

Two lines, written immediately after they leave, before you write anything else:

- The one thing that most got in their way.
- The one thing you would change first because of this session.

Write these before reading anyone else's session notes. Once you have read three sessions the fourth
starts confirming what you already think, and these two lines are the only defence against that.
