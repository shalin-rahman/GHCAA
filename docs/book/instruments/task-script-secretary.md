# Task script: General Secretary

Five tasks, about twenty-five minutes. The General Secretary's work is admission, notice and record,
so these tasks follow those rather than the menu structure.

---

## Task 1. Admit a member

**Start state:** signed in as an administrator, with at least one application pending.

> Someone has applied to join. Decide whether to admit them, and record the decision.

**Success:** the application reviewed, the supporting documents opened, a decision recorded, and the
tier assigned.

**Watch for:** whether they can see enough to decide without leaving the platform; whether they
understand that the tier is theirs to assign rather than the applicant's to request; whether the
thirty-day limit in DC-08 is visible anywhere. If nothing on the screen tells them how long an
application has been waiting, that is a requirement gap and should be written up as one.

---

## Task 2. Turn one down

**Start state:** a second application, with something wrong in it.

> This one does not qualify. Handle it properly.

**Success:** the application rejected with a recorded reason.

**Watch for:** what they expect to happen next for the applicant, and whether they mention appeal.
DC-07 gives a right of appeal within fifteen working days. If the Secretary does not think of it and
the platform does not raise it, say so plainly in the write-up.

---

## Task 3. Tell everyone something

> The reunion venue has changed. Make sure the members know.

**Success:** a notice published and visible to members, and the Secretary able to say who will see
it.

**Watch for:** whether they can tell the difference between a notice and a news item; whether they
expect it to reach people who do not open the platform.

---

## Task 4. Run the reunion

> Set up the reunion so members can register for it, with a cap on numbers.

**Success:** the event created with date, venue, capacity and registration open.

**Watch for:** whether the capacity behaviour is understood before it is needed, and whether the
date fields behave as expected on their device.

---

## Task 5. Publish a rule change

> The Executive Committee has approved an amendment to the constitution. Publish the new version so
> members read the current rules and not the old ones.

**Success:** the new version published and the earlier one retained rather than replaced.

**Watch for:** whether they expect the old version to disappear. DC-16 keeps superseded versions, and
whether the person doing the publishing understands that is the point of this task. This is the
clearest governance-boundary observation available from a non-technical participant and it feeds
§12.8 directly.

---

## Closing
