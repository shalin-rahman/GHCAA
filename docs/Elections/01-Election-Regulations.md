# 1. Purpose

These Regulations prescribe the detailed procedures governing the election of the Executive Committee of HARAGANGIAN.

These Regulations derive authority from Article V of the Constitution.

---

# 2. Scope

These Regulations apply to

• Election Commission
• Election Officials
• Candidates
• Members
• Observers
• Technical Administrators
• Polling Officers
• Appeal Tribunal

• Name

• Batch

• Membership Type

Removal Objection

# 11. Voting

Voting shall be

Secret.

Every voter

One vote per position.

Identity verification mandatory.

Voting methods

• Paper Ballot

• Electronic

• Hybrid

Proxy voting prohibited unless approved by Constitution.

# 12. Electronic Voting

System shall support

• OTP Verification

• Email Verification

• Unique Login

• Duplicate Prevention

• Vote Encryption

• Immutable Audit Logs

• Database Integrity Verification
