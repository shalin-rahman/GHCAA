# 1. Purpose

This manual prescribes the official procedures for conducting manual paper ballot elections.

Every Polling Officer, Observer and Election Commissioner shall strictly follow these procedures.

Deviation is permitted only by written order of the Chief Election Commissioner.

---

# 2. Polling Team

• Presiding Officer
• Polling Officer
• Assistant Polling Officer
• Queue Controller
• Observer
• Security Officer (if required)

# 5. Before Poll Opens

The Presiding Officer shall:

1. Display empty ballot box.

2. Allow observers to inspect.

3. Allow candidate agents to inspect.

4. Lock ballot box.

5. Apply numbered security seals.

6. Record seal numbers.

7. Complete Poll Opening Certificate.

8. Obtain signatures from:

- Presiding Officer
- Polling Officer
- Observer(s)
- Candidate Agents (if present)

No polling shall commence until these steps are complete.

---

# 6. Poll Opening Procedure

At the scheduled opening time:

1. Declare polling open.

2. Admit voters in an orderly manner.

3. Verify voter identity.

4. Confirm voter appears on Final Voter List.

5. Mark voter as issued ballot.

6. Obtain voter signature or thumb impression.

7. Stamp official ballot.

8. Issue one ballot paper.

9. Direct voter to voting booth.

10. Voter folds ballot and deposits it in ballot box.

11. Thank voter and direct them to exit.

---

# 7. Voting Procedure

Each voter shall:

Step 1
Present valid identification.

Step 2
Identity verified.

Step 3
Membership verified.

Step 4
Receive official ballot.

Step 5
Vote secretly.

Step 6
Fold ballot.

Step 7
Deposit ballot.

Step 8
Exit polling station.

No one may accompany a voter unless assistance is approved by the Presiding Officer.

# 8. Special Cases

## Name Missing

Voter not permitted to vote.

Record in Incident Register.

---

## Duplicate Attempt

Do not issue ballot.

Notify Commission immediately.

Record incident.

---

## Damaged Ballot

Mark

"SPOILED"

Retain original.

Issue replacement.

Record in Spoiled Ballot Register.

---

## Assisted Voting

Allowed only for:

• Physical disability

• Visual impairment

Assistant must sign Assisted Voting Register.

---

## Disorder

Suspend polling if necessary.

Notify Chief Election Commissioner.

Record all actions.

# 12. Vote Counting Procedure

Move to counting area.

Open ballot box.

Count total ballots.

Compare with

Ballot Issue Register.

If mismatch

Investigate immediately.

Sort ballots by office.

Count votes aloud.

Independent tally maintained by second officer.

Observers may watch.

Candidate agents may observe.

No one may touch ballots except authorized officials.

---

# 13. Invalid Ballots

Ballot shall be rejected if:

No official stamp.

No official signature (if required).

Marks for multiple candidates where only one permitted.

No clear voter intention.

Writing identifies voter.

Ballot torn so vote unclear.

Ballot altered.

Every rejected ballot

Numbered

Recorded

Reason documented.

Rejected ballots stored separately.

---

# 14. Recount

Recount may occur when:

Margin extremely close.

Counting error suspected.

Commission orders recount.

Tribunal orders recount.

Entire recount follows same procedure.

Reason documented.

Final count certified.

# Appendix C
Golden Rules

✓ Never leave ballot box unattended.

✓ Never count before poll officially closes.

✓ Never issue unstamped ballots.

✓ Never discuss unofficial results.

✓ Never allow campaigning inside polling station.

✓ Never destroy election materials.

✓ Record every incident immediately.

✓ Obtain signatures on every certificate before proceeding to the next stage.
