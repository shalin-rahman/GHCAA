Election Forms Review

All 50 election forms, laid out the way the platform prints them: organisation header, the record code, a key and value block, the body, signature lines, the seal and the verification QR footer. The Regulations §18 list (ER-01 to ER-35) sets the numbers. The 15 forms that exist only in the templates take a letter after the form they follow.

Sources: Regulations §18, templates 05, 06 and 07, and the form inventory in spec 023. Drawn and decided 2026-10-20. The owner approved every layout shown here on that date. Sheets marked NEW LAYOUT are the 10 forms the Regulations name without a field list; their layouts were drawn here and approved with the rest.

Numbering for the 15 template-only forms

ER-01 to ER-35 keep their Regulations numbers. Each template-only form takes a letter after the form it follows, so the printed pack reads in the order the forms are used. Two other schemes were considered and not chosen: ER-36 to ER-50 appended at the end, and a separate EA-01 annex series.

Old platform codes

The platform printed 16 forms under template numbers before this change. Each old code still opens the same form on the document link and on the verify page, so QR codes already printed keep working. The sheet prints the new code.

Decisions, 2026-10-20

  1. Numbering. Regulations numbers ER-01 to ER-35, with a letter suffix for the 15 template-only forms (table above).
  2. Old codes. The 16 codes printed before this change still open the right form (table above).
  3. ER-01 and ER-02 dates. Stored on the election: announced on, nomination opens and closes, scrutiny, withdrawal deadline, final list date, polling opens and closes, campaign opens and closes, silence period, venue, notice reference number and recount window end.
  4. Recount. One stored record per filing holds the request and the decision: seat, applicant, grounds, received time, decision, reason and who decided. ER-28 prints the request, ER-28A the decision, and ER-28B compares the original count with the recount.
  5. Handover. Moves to ER-34 with the 15-item checklist from template 07, and each tick is stored. ER-33 is the Election Completion Report, as in the Regulations.
  6. ER-17 scrutiny ticks. Seven ticks stored on each nomination: membership verified, voting member, minimum period, founding requirement, documents complete, fee paid, declaration signed. Any commissioner may tick, and the sheet prints who ticked and when.
  7. Title-only matches. All six pairs are one form: ER-08 with 05 ER-07, ER-17 with 05 ER-11, ER-27 with 07 ER-23, ER-29 with 07 ER-30, ER-31 with 07 ER-34, ER-32 with 07 ER-35.
  8. Signers. ER-03 is signed by the Selection Committee chair and one member. ER-20 stays a separate form from the Code of Conduct acceptance ECC-01.
  9. ER-13. Reuses the ER-05 neutrality text plus the lines on not holding political office. Candidates sign ER-13; officials sign ER-05.
  10. Layouts. Approved as shown below.
  11. Vote receipt. Made on the server as a PDF with Bengali text. It prints the receipt code, the election and the time, and never the choices.
  12. Build order. ER-04, ER-05, ER-23 and ER-24 are built first, because their data is already stored. The stored fields above come with a migration that waits for the preprod migration gate.
